Multimedia Denoising & Restoration

A Student-Authored Learning Hub for Introduction to Multimedia Communications (Group 02)

Project Overview & Learning Objectives

Digital images, audio, and video frequently suffer from noise, blur, and compression artifacts during capture and transmission. This learning hub explores how multimedia degradation occurs and compares traditional signal processing filters with modern AI-based restoration models.

  • Identify common types of spatial and temporal noise across image, audio, and video signals.
  • Compare classical filtering algorithms with deep learning restoration models in terms of detail retention and complexity.
  • Evaluate restoration performance using objective metrics (PSNR, SSIM) alongside human perceptual quality.
